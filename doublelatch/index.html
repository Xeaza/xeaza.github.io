Using Double Latch

Getting started and adding more babies

Tap Add your baby on the welcome screen. Add a name, choose a color, and select Nursing, Bottle feeding, or Both. A photo is optional.

Use More → Babies to add or edit profiles. Double Latch supports one, two, or more babies. You can run up to two nursing timers at the same time, one for each side.

Nursing timers and the suggested side

On Tracking, tap Left or Right to start a nursing timer, then Stop when the feed is finished. Each baby has a separate card.

The suggested side is the opposite side of the last completed nursing feed. It is a reminder based on your records. Bottle entries do not change it.

The automatic timer limit starts at 30 minutes and can be changed in Settings. Changes apply to future timers. You can correct a completed feed in History.

Bottles and diaper changes

In Bottle feeding or Both mode, use Log bottle to record the amount consumed. You can choose mL or US fl oz in Settings and optionally record breast milk, formula, or mixed milk.

The water-drop button records a wet diaper. The poop button records a diaper that is both wet and soiled. To record poop only, change a time, or add a note, use a diaper entry in History. Occasional off-mode entries are available through Add entry.

Finding, correcting, or deleting an entry

Open More → History, or tap a baby's Tracking card to see that baby's history. Choose All, Feeds, or Diapers, then tap an entry to edit it.

Swipe an individual completed entry to delete it. An Undo button appears briefly after deletion. Running nursing timers must be stopped before their records can be edited or deleted.

Backups, CSV exports, and moving to another device

Each device keeps its own records. Double Latch does not automatically sync between devices or caregivers.

In More → Settings, you can export separate nursing, bottle, and diaper CSV files for viewing or sharing. CSV files cannot be restored into the app.

To move your records, stop all active nursing timers and choose Save full backup. Save that file somewhere you can access outside the app, then restore it in Double Latch on the other device. A full backup includes profiles, optional photos, records, and preferences.

Restoring a backup replaces the records already on that device. Save a backup of those records first if you want to keep them. Deleting the app removes its local data, so keep a full backup before reinstalling.

Archiving a baby

Open the baby's details and choose Archive profile. Stop any active timer for that baby first. Archiving removes the profile from Tracking and keeps its history. You can restore an archived profile in Babies.

Optional analytics, retention, and deletion requests

If your app version includes More → Settings → Share usage & diagnostics, you can choose whether to send feature-use events and crash diagnostics to PostHog. Sharing is on by default when no preference has been saved. You can turn it off in Settings, and the app works with it off. An existing off preference is preserved. Baby names, photos, notes, amounts, and saved care records are excluded. Backups do not enable this setting.

Turning sharing off stops new collection; it does not erase data already received. To ask about retention or deletion, email doublelatch@gmail.com. Because there is no account, we may need limited technical information to locate device-associated diagnostics. Please do not send baby records or backups. The privacy policy explains retention and deletion separately from local records.

Privacy and account questions

No account is required. Double Latch has no ads, and we do not operate a backend that receives your saved care records. App versions with Share usage & diagnostics offer optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics, on by default when no preference has been saved. You can turn sharing off in Settings. Optional photos and records are stored on your device. You choose whether to export or share a file.
