Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Your feeding and diaper records are stored on your device. Double Latch has no account, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or developer-operated backend that receives those records.

This policy explains how Taylor Sanson ("we" or "us") handles information in connection with Double Latch and its support website.

Information you enter

The app stores the information you choose to add: baby names and profile colors, optional photos, nursing sides and times, bottle amounts and milk types, diaper changes and notes, and app preferences. It supports profiles for one, two, or more babies.

This information is kept in the app's local storage. We do not receive it automatically, use it to track you, sell it, or use it for advertising. There is no automatic caregiver sharing or device-to-device sync.

Optional photos and notifications

If you add a profile photo, you choose it with Apple's system photo picker. The app stores a smaller copy locally and does not request access to your entire photo library or camera.

If enabled, local notifications and Live Activities show feeding information on your device, including the Lock Screen. You can control their visibility and permissions in your device settings. Double Latch does not use a developer-operated remote notification server.

Exports, backups, and Apple services

You can choose to export CSV files or a full backup through the system sharing interface. Those files contain the records you export; full backups also include profiles, optional photos, and preferences. Where the file goes depends on the destination you select, such as Files, iCloud Drive, an email app, or another person.

Your device or iCloud backups may also include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Double Latch does not use CloudKit or its own server to sync your records.

Apple separately handles App Store or TestFlight distribution and may process usage or diagnostic information according to its own policies and your settings. Apple's services and any destination you choose for an exported file have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and deleting information

You can edit profiles and photos, correct or delete individual feeding and diaper records, and change app preferences. Archiving a profile keeps its history; it does not erase the records.

Deleting the app using your device's Delete App control removes its local app data. Offloading an app can retain its data. Exported files, copies shared with others, and device or iCloud backups are managed separately through their respective services and settings.

We cannot access, restore, or remotely delete information stored only on your device. Keep a full backup outside the app if you want to preserve your records before deleting the app or replacing a device.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain support records for as long as reasonably needed. Our email provider processes those communications as part of delivering email.

You do not need to send baby photos, identifiable feeding records, or backup files to ask for help. Contact taylorwrightsanson@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests about support correspondence.

This website

These support pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, embedded third-party content, or contact forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary request information needed to serve and protect the website under its own privacy practices.

Parents and caregivers

Double Latch is designed for parents and caregivers to record information about babies in their care. It does not directly solicit information from children. Information an adult chooses to enter about a baby follows the local-storage practices described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the date above, along with any required App Store privacy disclosures. The current version will be available on this page.
