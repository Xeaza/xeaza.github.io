Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Your feeding and diaper records are stored on your device. Double Latch has no account or advertising. App versions with optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics enable sharing by default when no preference has been saved. You can turn sharing off in Settings. Saved care records are not uploaded to a developer-operated backend.

This policy explains how we handle information in connection with Double Latch and its support website. Contact us using the email address below with privacy questions.

Information you enter

The app stores the information you choose to add: baby names and profile colors, optional photos, nursing sides and times, bottle amounts and milk types, diaper changes and notes, and app preferences. It supports profiles for one, two, or more babies.

This information is kept in the app's local storage. We do not receive it automatically, use it to track you, sell it, or use it for advertising. There is no automatic caregiver sharing or device-to-device sync.

Optional photos and notifications

If you add a profile photo, you choose it with Apple's system photo picker. The app stores a smaller copy locally and does not request access to your entire photo library or camera.

If enabled, local notifications and Live Activities show feeding information on your device, including the Lock Screen. You can control their visibility and permissions in your device settings. Double Latch does not use a developer-operated remote notification server.

Exports, backups, and Apple services

You can choose to export CSV files or a full backup through the system sharing interface. Those files contain the records you export; full backups also include profiles, optional photos, and preferences. Where the file goes depends on the destination you select, such as Files, iCloud Drive, an email app, or another person.

Your device or iCloud backups may also include app data, depending on your Apple settings. Double Latch does not use CloudKit or its own server to sync your records.

Apple separately handles App Store or TestFlight distribution and may process usage or diagnostic information according to its own policies and your settings. Apple's services and any destination you choose for an exported file have their own privacy practices.

Optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics

App versions with the Share usage & diagnostics setting offer optional analytics and crash diagnostics. Sharing is on by default when no preference has been saved. You can turn it off in Settings at any time, and an existing off preference is preserved. While sharing is enabled, PostHog processes feature-use events (such as starting a timer or saving an entry), event times, a randomly generated device identifier, app and operating-system versions, and crash stack addresses to help us improve reliability and understand feature use. Even without names, these device-associated events can indicate use of nursing, bottle, or diaper features.

We exclude baby names and care-record identifiers, photos, notes, nursing side, diaper contents, bottle amounts, saved care records, backup contents, exception messages, and screen recordings. Automatic screen and interaction capture, session replay, surveys, and location enrichment are disabled. We do not use this information for advertising, data brokerage, or tracking across other companies' apps or websites. PostHog processes this data in its US service; see PostHog's privacy policy.

You can disable sharing in Settings at any time to stop new collection. This does not erase information already received. Contact us using the address below for questions about retention or deletion; without an account, we may not be able to associate a request with an anonymous device identifier. App versions without this setting do not use PostHog. Restoring a care backup does not enable sharing. Support correspondence remains separate.

Retention and deletion of optional diagnostics

We use optional usage and diagnostic information to understand feature use and investigate reliability problems. Analytics and crash events follow the event-retention settings for our PostHog project; diagnostic logs follow separate log-retention settings. Retention depends on the service settings and plan. Expiration and permanent deletion are separate processes, so expired information may remain while deletion is processed. We do not promise that disabling sharing or deleting the app immediately erases information already received.

To ask about retention, access, or deletion, email doublelatch@gmail.com. We will explain what we can locate and arrange deletion of associated information where we can identify it. Double Latch has no account and does not attach your name or email address to analytics, so an email address alone may not let us locate a device's diagnostic information. We may ask for limited technical information to identify the relevant data; please do not send baby names, photos, feeding history, or backup files.

Turning sharing off stops new collection. A deletion request is a separate step. Deletion can take time to complete in the service, and we will distinguish a submitted request from confirmed completion. Support emails are handled separately as described below.

Your choices and deleting information

You can edit profiles and photos, correct or delete individual feeding and diaper records, and change app preferences. Archiving a profile keeps its history; it does not erase the records.

Deleting the app using your device's Delete App control removes its local app data. Offloading an app can retain its data. Exported files, copies shared with others, and device or iCloud backups are managed separately through their respective services and settings.

We cannot access, restore, or remotely delete information stored only on your device. Keep a full backup outside the app if you want to preserve your records before deleting the app or replacing a device.

Contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain support records for as long as reasonably needed. Our email provider processes those communications as part of delivering email.

You do not need to send baby photos, identifiable feeding records, or backup files to ask for help. Contact doublelatch@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests about support correspondence.

This website

These support pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, embedded third-party content, or contact forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary request information needed to serve and protect the website under its own privacy practices.

Parents and caregivers

Double Latch is designed for parents and caregivers to record information about babies in their care. It does not directly solicit information from children. Information an adult chooses to enter about a baby follows the local-storage practices described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the date above, along with any required App Store privacy disclosures. The current version will be available on this page.
